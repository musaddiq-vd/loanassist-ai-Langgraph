# Personal Loan Policy (sample)

> Sample content. Replace with your real personal loan policy.

## Documents required
- PAN card and Aadhaar card
- Last 3 months' salary slips
- Last 3 months' bank statements

## Key terms
- Loan amount: up to INR 25 lakh
- Tenure: 12 to 60 months
- No collateral required
- Foreclosure: allowed after 12 EMIs, charge up to 4% of the outstanding principal
