# Eligibility and Application Process (sample, all loan types)

> Sample content. Replace with your real policy.

## Eligibility guidelines
- Minimum age 21, maximum age 60 at loan maturity (65 for self-employed)
- Minimum monthly income: INR 25,000 for salaried applicants
- Total EMIs, including the new loan, should not exceed 50% of monthly income
- A CIBIL score of 750 or above is preferred
- A CIBIL score below 650 is sent for manual review by a loan officer

## Application process
1. Submit the application with the required documents
2. Document verification and credit check
3. Property valuation (home loans only)
4. Sanction letter issued
5. Loan agreement signed and amount disbursed

## Important
The final loan decision is always made by a loan officer. This assistant does not approve or reject loans.
