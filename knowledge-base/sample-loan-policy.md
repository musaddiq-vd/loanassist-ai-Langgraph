# Sample Loan Policy (demo content)

> This is sample content so the app works on first deploy. Replace or add your
> real loan policy documents (PDF, DOCX, MD, TXT) in this `knowledge-base/` folder
> and run `.\deploy.ps1` again.

## Home Loan

**Documents required**
- Identity proof: PAN card and Aadhaar card
- Address proof: Aadhaar, passport, or utility bill
- Income proof (salaried): last 3 months' salary slips and Form 16
- Income proof (self-employed): last 2 years' ITR and audited financial statements
- Last 6 months' bank statements
- Property documents: sale agreement, title deed, and approved building plan

**Key terms**
- Tenure: up to 30 years
- Loan-to-value: up to 80% of the property value
- Processing fee: up to 0.5% of the loan amount

## Personal Loan

**Documents required**
- PAN card and Aadhaar card
- Last 3 months' salary slips
- Last 3 months' bank statements

**Key terms**
- Loan amount: up to INR 25 lakh
- Tenure: 12 to 60 months
- No collateral required

## Eligibility Guidelines

- Minimum age 21, maximum age 60 at loan maturity (65 for self-employed)
- Minimum monthly income: INR 25,000 for salaried applicants
- Total EMIs, including the new loan, should not exceed 50% of monthly income
- A CIBIL score of 750 or above is preferred
- A CIBIL score below 650 is sent for manual review by a loan officer

## Application Process

1. Submit the application with the required documents
2. Document verification and credit check
3. Property valuation (home loans only)
4. Sanction letter issued
5. Loan agreement signed and amount disbursed

## Important

The final loan decision is always made by a loan officer. This assistant does not
approve or reject loans.
