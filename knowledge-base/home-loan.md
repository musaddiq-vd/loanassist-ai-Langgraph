# Home Loan Policy (sample)

> Sample content. Replace with your real home loan policy.

## Documents required
- Identity proof: PAN card and Aadhaar card
- Address proof: Aadhaar, passport, or utility bill
- Income proof (salaried): last 3 months' salary slips and Form 16
- Income proof (self-employed): last 2 years' ITR and audited financial statements
- Last 6 months' bank statements
- Property documents: sale agreement, title deed, and approved building plan

## Key terms
- Tenure: up to 30 years
- Loan-to-value: up to 80% of the property value
- Processing fee: up to 0.5% of the loan amount
- Prepayment: no charges on floating-rate home loans for individuals
